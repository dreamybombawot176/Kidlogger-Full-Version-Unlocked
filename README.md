## Main Features
<a id="main-features"></a>

### Core Functionality
- **Keystroke Logging** — Capture every keystroke made on the computer, allowing parents to see what their children are typing, whether in documents or online chats.
- **Clipboard Monitoring** — Track clipboard activity to know what information is being copied and pasted by the user, providing insights into their online research.
- **File and Drive Usage** — Monitor the use of files, folders, and drives, including USB drives, to ensure that children are not accessing inappropriate materials.
- **User Control** — Control monitoring for all users or select specific accounts, giving tailored oversight based on the child's age and needs.
- **Website Tracking** — Record URLs of websites visited to ensure safe browsing habits are maintained while children are online.

- **Screen Capture** — Take periodic screenshots to visually monitor what children are doing on the computer, providing a real-time view of their activities.
- **Customizable Settings** — Adjust the settings to determine how often to capture screen images or audio, allowing for flexibility in monitoring.
